About Premji Invest

Premji Invest is a global investment firm that partners with ambitious entrepreneurs and category-defining companies. They invest across financial services, technology, consumer, healthcare, and industrial sectors, with a strong belief in the transformative power of innovation.

Premji Invest has a portfolio of 160-plus investments and more than $15 billion in evergreen capital and compounding. Guided by long-term thinking, integrity, and excellence, they back businesses that are building the future and creating lasting value.
