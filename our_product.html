Our Brands

From homes to salons, trusted by millions worldwide

Streax

For over two decades, Streax has empowered millions of Indians with innovative hair colour and care solutions designed for everyday confidence. Combining quality, performance, and affordability, Streax makes beautiful, healthy hair accessible to all.

Learn More

Streax Professional

Trusted by hair experts across the world, Streax Professional delivers advanced colour and care solutions powered by international technology and salon expertise. It enables professionals to create exceptional hair transformations with confidence.

Learn More

Vasmol

Rooted in Indian beauty traditions, Vasmol blends the goodness of natural ingredients with trusted hair colour expertise. For generations, it has been known for its henna-based solutions, helping consumers achieve long-lasting care and confidence.

Learn More

Florozone

Florozone offers accessible personal care solutions crafted for the everyday needs of modern Indian families. With gentle, effective formulations, it brings freshness, comfort, and care to daily routines.
