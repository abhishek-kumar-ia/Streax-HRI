Powering India’s Fastest-Growing Premium Beauty Brands

About HRI

For over 50 years, Hygienic Research Institute Private Limited (HRIPL) has been creating trusted beauty and personal care solutions for Indian consumers. Today, we are one of India’s fastest-growing beauty companies, with a strong presence across haircare, hair colour, skincare, and professional beauty categories.

Backed by six manufacturing facilities and a team of 700+ professionals, we combine scientific innovation with quality excellence to deliver products that consumers trust. Our portfolio includes leading brands such as Vasmol, Streax, and Florozone, alongside global partnerships with Carin Cosmetics and Lisap.

Our Growth Story

From a single manufacturing unit in Mumbai in the 1950s to a strong pan-India presence today, HRIPL has grown steadily into a leading beauty and personal care company driven by purpose and innovation.

With six state-of-the-art manufacturing facilities across India and a team of 700+ employees, we continue to scale with a strong focus on quality, efficiency, and consumer trust.

Know Our Story

The Beginning

In 1950, Dr S.S. Nishat founded the Hygienic Research Institute (HRI), laying the foundation for India's most loved hair care brands.

Rooted in Science

Mrs Ritika Chhabra laid the foundation for HRI's Research & Development (R&D) function, keeping science at the heart of HRI.

Launching Streax

HRI reimagined hair colour as self-expression, giving birth to Streax. The hair colour brand loved by generations in India.

Our Investor: Partners in Growth

Strong fundamentals, consumer trust built over decades, and a team with deep category expertise, HRI is positioned to define the next chapter of India's beauty industry.
